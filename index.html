About

Design and engineering, from the same starting point.

I started in Computer Science, not design — which means I came to UI/UX already thinking in systems, states, and constraints rather than just screens.

That background shapes how I work now: I research and structure a problem the way an engineer scopes one, then design the interface the way a craftsperson finishes it. I'm as comfortable in a usability test as I am reading through a component's variant properties in Figma, or writing the HTML/CSS/JS that gets a design into a browser.

I'm a fresh Computer Science graduate looking for a UI/UX or product design role where that combination — research-backed design decisions that a development team can actually build — is the point, not a bonus.
