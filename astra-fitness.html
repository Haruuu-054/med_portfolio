Astra Fitness

Designing a bold, focused fitness landing page that communicates Astra Fitness’s value, builds motivation, and guides visitors toward taking action.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Team
Individual Project
Platform
Responsive Website
Tools
Figma · HTML · CSS
01

The problem

Fitness websites can easily become overloaded with information, imagery, and competing calls to action. Astra Fitness needed a clearer visual hierarchy that could communicate energy, training, and value quickly without overwhelming the visitor.

The challenge was to make the experience feel energetic and aspirational while keeping the interface easy to scan. The design needed to balance strong visual impact with clear navigation and conversion-focused content.

02

Who it's for

Primary

The motivated gym-goer

  • Wants a motivating and visually engaging fitness experience
  • Looks for clear information about training and the brand
  • Needs an obvious next step without having to search for it
Secondary

The fitness-focused visitor

  • Explores fitness content and services before committing
  • Responds to strong visuals, concise messaging, and clear benefits
  • Wants the site to feel modern, energetic, and easy to navigate
03

Goals

Beyond looking visually strong, the landing page needed to turn interest into action through clear messaging, hierarchy, and calls to action.

04

Research & insights

Research focused on understanding fitness landing-page patterns, visual expectations in the fitness space, and how content hierarchy can move a visitor from first impression to action.

Finding
Fitness landing pages often compete for attention with large imagery, bold typography, and multiple content sections.
Implication
A strong visual direction needs to support the message rather than compete with it.
Action
Made the hero message, value proposition, and primary CTA the dominant elements of the page.
Finding
Many fitness experiences rely heavily on visuals, but too much visual density can make the main message harder to find.
Implication
The most important information should be immediately scannable without forcing visitors through unnecessary navigation.
Action
Used a strong landing-page hierarchy so the brand message and primary action are immediately visible.
05

Structure & flow

As a landing page, the structure was kept intentionally focused: each section supports the visitor’s journey from discovering Astra Fitness to taking action.

Hero / Value Proposition
→
Fitness Experience
→
Benefits / Content
→
CTA / Conversion

No unnecessary detours were added to the page. Every section was designed to support the visual story, communicate value, or move the visitor toward the next action.

06

Design exploration

The visual direction explored how to combine Astra Fitness’s energetic brand character with a clean landing-page hierarchy. The design prioritized impact without sacrificing readability or conversion.

Option A — Rejected

A content-heavy layout with too much information above the fold. Visually interesting, but it weakened the primary message and made the next action less obvious.

Option B — Selected

A bold hero-led layout that establishes the brand immediately, communicates the core value proposition, and uses clear CTAs to guide the visitor through the page.

07

Key design decisions

Message before detail
The hero communicates what Astra Fitness is about before asking the visitor to process supporting information.
Hierarchy, not clutter
Content is grouped and prioritized so users can quickly understand the page without competing messages fighting for attention.
Energy through color
A dark foundation paired with Astra Fitness’s neon green accent creates a high-energy fitness aesthetic while keeping key actions visually prominent.

10

Outcome

Astra Fitness demonstrates the ability to translate a strong visual identity into a responsive landing page with clear hierarchy, persuasive content, and conversion-focused interactions.

This was a design project focused on demonstrating landing-page design, responsive thinking, visual hierarchy, and brand-driven UI decisions rather than a shipped production website.

11

Reflection

What I learned

Designing Astra Fitness reinforced that a landing page does not need to show everything at once. Strong hierarchy and intentional sectioning make the experience feel more focused and persuasive.

What I'd improve

Given more time, I would validate the landing page with fitness-focused users and test whether the messaging, imagery, and CTA hierarchy communicate the value proposition quickly enough.

What I'd do next

I would next explore more interaction states, refine the responsive behavior across breakpoints, and test different CTA placements to improve the overall conversion flow.
